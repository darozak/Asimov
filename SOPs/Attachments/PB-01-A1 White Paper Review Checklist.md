# PB-01-A1: White Paper Review Checklist

> **Attachment:** PB-01-A1 — Appendix
> **Parent SOP:** [PB-01](../Procedures/PB-01%20Researching%2C%20Drafting%2C%20Editing%2C%20and%20Publishing%20White%20Papers.md)
> **Use:** Applied during the editing stage (PB-01 §5) and again before the paper
> moves to Published. Record the result — each item `PASS` / `FAIL` / `N/A`, with a
> note for every `FAIL` — and resolve all failures before advancing the paper.

## A. Structure and metadata

- [ ] The paper follows the [PB-01-T1](./PB-01-T1%20White%20Paper%20Template.md) section set; no section deleted (non-applicable sections marked with a reason).
- [ ] ID is `WP-YYYY-NN`, unique in `White Papers/`, and matches the filename `<ID> <Title>.md`.
- [ ] Metadata block complete: ID, Title, Authors (AI contribution noted), Status, Version, Last updated, Board review, Supersedes / Superseded by.
- [ ] Abstract is 150–250 words and states question, method, findings, and recommendation.
- [ ] Changelog has a row for the current version.

## B. Research quality

- [ ] Every factual claim is traceable to a numbered entry in **References** via an inline `[n]` citation.
- [ ] Sources are current and authoritative for the claim they support; date range of evidence is stated in **Methods and Sources**.
- [ ] Primary sources are used where the claim is load-bearing (statutes quoted from the text, data from the originating dataset).
- [ ] Web sources have an access date; links resolve.
- [ ] Findings are separated from the authors' interpretation.
- [ ] Counterarguments and alternative readings are addressed in **Discussion**.
- [ ] **Limitations** honestly states gaps, assumptions, and any conflicts of interest.

## C. Argument and relevance

- [ ] The introduction states the question, its relevance to the Asimov Project mission, and the scope.
- [ ] Each recommendation is tied to a specific finding.
- [ ] Recommendations addressed to the board name the decision being requested.
- [ ] Implications for the program, legislative strategy, and the business plan are addressed in **Discussion**.
- [ ] If the paper should inform the business plan, a follow-up issue or AD-02 revision is noted.

## D. Writing and accessibility

- [ ] Prose is clear; defined terms are defined on first use; acronyms expanded once.
- [ ] Tables and figures have captions and are referenced from the text.
- [ ] GitHub-flavored Markdown throughout; renders correctly (headings, tables, lists, links).
- [ ] No unresolved template placeholders or editorial `TODO` markers.
- [ ] Spelling and grammar checked.

## E. Compliance

- [ ] Content stays within the organization's advocacy and lobbying limits (see [AD-02](../Procedures/AD-02%20Creating%20and%20Maintaining%20the%20Business%20Plan.md) §13); no statement commits the organization to prohibited activity.
- [ ] Third-party material is quoted and attributed, not reproduced beyond fair use.
- [ ] Any personal data or interview material has the necessary consent.

- **NOT READY** — one or more `FAIL`; list them.
