## 3. Methods and Sources

*How the research was conducted: what was reviewed, how sources were selected, any
analysis performed, and the date range of the evidence. List the nature of sources

## 4. Findings

*The evidence and analysis, organized by sub-question. Separate what the evidence

legislative strategy, and for the business plan. Address counterarguments and
alternative readings of the evidence.*

## 6. Limitations

*What this paper does not establish: gaps in the evidence, assumptions made,
conflicts of interest, and how much weight the conclusions can bear.*
